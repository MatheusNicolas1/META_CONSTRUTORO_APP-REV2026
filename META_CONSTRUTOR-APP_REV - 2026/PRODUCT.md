# Meta Construtor Product Context

register: brand

## Product Purpose

Meta Construtor is a web platform for construction companies that need a single place to manage jobsites, RDOs, checklists, teams, documents, integrations, reports and operational visibility. The marketing surface must make the product easy to understand for owners, directors, engineers and managers who are comparing tools to reduce rework and gain control over field execution.

## Primary Users

- Construction company owners and directors who want operational control and commercial confidence before adopting a SaaS product.
- Engineers and project managers who need daily visibility into job progress, RDOs, teams, tasks and issues.
- Administrative and operations teams that need documents, reports and accountability in one platform.
- Technical buyers who care about integrations, API, security, LGPD and reliable support.

## Buying Context

Visitors usually arrive with practical pressure: delayed jobsites, scattered documentation, unreliable field records, slow reporting, or difficulty standardizing teams. They need direct proof that the product is concrete, organized and trustworthy.

## Brand Voice

- Direct
- Operational
- Trustworthy
- Clear
- Practical

Avoid vague SaaS language, inflated promises, decorative buzzwords and generic transformation claims without proof. Prefer concrete construction language: obra, RDO, checklist, equipe, documento, relatorio, atraso, retrabalho, aprovacao, medicao and rotina de campo.

## Strategic Principles

- Lead with the operational problem, then show the product as the organizing system.
- Make the first viewport answer: what it is, who it is for, why it matters and what to do next.
- Use proof over decoration: product screens, workflows, concrete outcomes, security and support details.
- Keep marketing pages indexable and semantically clean.
- Keep authenticated product routes separate from public marketing routes.

## Anti-References

- Generic SaaS landing pages built from repeated icon cards.
- Purple or cyan AI-style gradients.
- Gradient text in headlines.
- Thick side accent borders on cards or legal sections.
- Decorative glassmorphism and glow-heavy dark sections.
- Claims such as "revolutionary" without concrete evidence.

## Key Public Surfaces

- `/home`: main landing page and organic search entry.
- `/preco`: plan comparison and purchase decision.
- `/sobre`: trust, company context and credibility.
- `/contato`: sales, support and partnership conversion.
- `/blog`: educational search acquisition.
- `/central-ajuda`: support and long-tail search.
- `/documentacao` and `/api`: technical trust and integrations.
- Legal pages: trust, compliance and discoverability.
